# SOP: Short payments and freight deductions

- A short payment is a remittance below the invoice total with a deduction noted on the remittance advice.
- Check the customer's contract: if freight is billable, the deduction is not valid and the balance is collected with a courteous follow-up citing the contract clause.
- If the contract says freight is not billable, credit the freight amount and close the balance; do not argue.
- Unapplied cash without a remittance memo: ask AP which invoice the payment covers before applying it.
