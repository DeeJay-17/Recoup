# SOP: Handling invoice disputes

1. Confirm the discrepancy with evidence before offering anything: compare the invoice against the customer PO, the delivery proof and the contract price list. Reconciliation output is authoritative for amounts.
2. Pricing disputes: credit the difference between invoiced and PO/contract price for the affected quantity, plus tax. Never credit below the reconciled amount and never above it.
3. Quantity disputes: credit the units not delivered according to the signed delivery proof; if the customer still wants the goods, raise a re-bill after re-delivery.
4. Duplicate invoices: void the later invoice in full when an earlier invoice for the same PO and lines is already paid.
5. Credit memos up to the autonomy threshold are issued automatically on confirmed disputes with PO evidence; larger ones require an analyst or manager approval.
6. Always send the customer a resolution notice with the credit memo reference and the remaining balance, and thread it under the original conversation.
