# SOP: Customer outreach

- Email only active accounts-payable contacts; contacts flagged inactive have left the company.
- Use approved templates for routine outreach (PO request, re-send invoice, payment reminder); free-form emails are reviewed by an analyst.
- Tone is courteous and factual. Never threaten, never use capitals for emphasis, never mention legal action without a manager.
- Follow-up cadence when there is no reply: 3 days, then 7 days, then 14 days, then hand the case to a human.
- Customers that require a purchase order on every invoice will not pay without one; ask for the PO before anything else.
