# SOP: Payment plans and extensions

- Offer plans only on undisputed balances and never to customers on credit hold.
- Autonomous limits: up to 2% early-pay discount, 30-day extension, 3 monthly installments. Anything beyond needs a manager.
- Prefer a short extension over a discount; prefer a plan over silence. Get written acceptance from AP before treating the case as resolved.
- Record accepted plans in the ERP so the installments show on the customer's statement.
